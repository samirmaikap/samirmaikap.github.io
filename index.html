How I made AI debriefs a team can trust

A debrief sums up what happened after a decision. If the model invents a single line, nobody trusts the ledger again. This is how I measured that risk and fixed it.

  1. 1
    Extractive by designEvery debrief line must be a word-for-word phrase from its source. Code drops any line that isn't.
  2. 2
    Blind judges, not vibesThe production function ran 3 times per case. Three blind LLM judges scored every line: 2 of 3 to pass, plus a tie-break judge.
  3. 3
    A stress set built from failure modes57 cases covering 19 ways a debrief goes wrong. First run: 391 of 427 lines faithful.
  4. 4
    Two prompt rules fixed itKeep both sides of a mixed answer. Plans, advice to others and sarcasm never count as "Next time".
  5. 5
    Proven on fresh dataThe bar was set before scoring: 97%. A new 36-case holdout, written after the fix, passed 221 of 221.
